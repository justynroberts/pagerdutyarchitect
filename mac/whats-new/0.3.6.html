What's new in PagerDuty Architect 0.3.6

  • Connect into any side: drag a line onto the side of a step you want and it stays there, so a step can take lines on two or more sides. Drop on a step's middle and the line takes the closest side.
  • The connector's Leaves from and Arrives at show Auto, with the side it chose, or the side you pinned. Pick Auto to unpin.
  • Fixes the app failing to open when Value assumptions were first saved by an early version. They're now repaired as the app loads; nothing is lost.
