What's new in PagerDuty Architect 0.3.3

  • Icon or logo: select a step and use Icon or logo in the inspector to pick an icon or a vendor logo (Datadog, Jira, GitHub and more).
  • PagerDuty capabilities: tick PagerDuty capability to show a step as a PagerDuty component, its own icon on the PagerDuty-green tile. A new PagerDuty catalogue section has Event Orchestration, AIOps, on-call paging, Incident Workflows, SRE Agent, Runbook Automation, Status Pages, Scribe and more.
  • Five more step types, each with its own shape: handoff, wait, document, system and sub-process, with ready-made steps in the catalogue.
  • Tidier connectors: lines take the closest sides for where your steps sit and redraw as you move them. Sides you pick in the inspector stay put; Arrange tidies everything.
