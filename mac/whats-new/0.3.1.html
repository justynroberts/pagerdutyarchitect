What's new in PagerDuty Architect 0.3.1

  • Value tab (was Commercial): the value told three ways, productivity gain, revenue protection and cost savings, each of which you can switch off. Add average and peak trading revenue for revenue protection, and switch on direct cost reductions such as redundant licences or outsourcing.
  • Export the Value tab as a 1920 x 1080 slide, or copy it as Markdown, from the File menu.
  • Hide the assumptions panel on the Value tab with the panel button at the top right. The PagerDuty logo sits at the bottom left.
  • Delete removes from the tab you're on; Shift Delete, or Delete from both, removes from Current and Future.
  • The app keeps its log across launches, so problems are easier to track down.
