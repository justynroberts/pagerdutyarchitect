What's new in PagerDuty Architect 0.3.12

  • Tidier Arrange: signals line up evenly in the first column, chains of steps run in straight lines, and a decision's main branch carries straight on with the others stacked beside it.
  • Hide removed steps: a new button in Future leaves out the steps automation replaces, instead of showing them crossed out, and lays Future out without them.
  • With times hidden, the stages bar no longer shows timings for Current and Future.
