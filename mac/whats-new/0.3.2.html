What's new in PagerDuty Architect 0.3.2

  • Stages under the top bar walk you through 1 Assumptions, 2 Current state, 3 Future state and 4 Value, each with its headline figure. Back and Next move one stage at a time; keys 1 to 4 jump straight to one.
  • Assumptions is a new first stage holding every input: customer, effort per run, incidents, rates, and the productivity, revenue and cost inputs for the Value sheet.
  • The Value page is results only, full width.
  • The metrics bar along the bottom is gone, so the diagram has more room.
  • Note: Current is now key 2 and Future key 3.
